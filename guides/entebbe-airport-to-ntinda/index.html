Getting from Entebbe Airport to Ntinda, Kampala

Updated 30 October 2026 · Furnished Apartments Uganda

Entebbe International Airport is Uganda's main airport, about 40 km from Ntinda by road. Most guests reach our apartments in roughly 1 to 1.5 hours, but the drive can take longer in Kampala's rush-hour traffic. Here's how to plan the trip.

The quick answer

  • Distance: about 40–45 km by road
  • Travel time: around 1 to 1.5 hours; allow up to 2 hours on weekday mornings and evenings
  • Easiest option: our airport pickup (extra cost), an airport taxi or an Uber
  • Expressway toll: UGX 5,000 per car, paid in cash (Ugandan shillings only) at the toll plaza

Your transport options

1. Book an airport pickup with us

If you're staying with us, the simplest option is our airport pickup, available at an extra cost. Send us your flight number and arrival time on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the price and arrange a driver to meet you at arrivals and bring you straight to your apartment. It's the least stressful choice after a long flight, especially if you land at night.

2. Uber

Uber operates at Entebbe Airport, and ride-hailing apps are widely used across Kampala. Open the app once you have mobile data or the airport WiFi, check the fare estimate, and follow the in-app directions to the pickup point. Fares change with demand and traffic, so compare the estimate before you confirm.

3. Airport taxi

Licensed taxis wait outside the arrivals hall. Agree the full fare to Ntinda before you get in, and confirm whether it includes the expressway toll. Having the price settled up front avoids surprises at the end of the trip.

4. Pre-booked transfer

Several online transfer companies let you book a car in advance with a fixed price, which is useful for groups or travellers with lots of luggage. These usually cost more than a local taxi, but the price and the driver are confirmed before you fly.

5. Public transport (budget option)

It's possible to reach Ntinda by shared minibus taxis, changing in central Kampala, but it's slow, can take close to 3 hours with connections, and isn't practical with suitcases. We don't recommend it for your arrival day.

The route

Most drivers take the Kampala–Entebbe Expressway, a toll road of about 51 km that bypasses the busy old Entebbe Road. From the Kampala end, drivers continue to Ntinda either through the city or along the Kampala Northern Bypass, depending on traffic. The expressway is the quickest part of the journey; most of the variation in travel time comes from traffic inside Kampala.

Tips for arriving at Entebbe

  • Carry some Ugandan shillings. The expressway toll is cash-only in shillings, and small notes are handy for tips and snacks. You'll find ATMs and currency exchange at the airport.
  • Get a local SIM card. Mobile network kiosks are available at the airport, and a local SIM makes Uber, WhatsApp and maps easy to use.
  • Avoid rush hour if you can. Weekday mornings and late afternoons are the slowest times to cross Kampala.
  • Share your arrival time with your host. We'll make sure the apartment is ready. Check-in is from 1:00 PM, and if you land early we'll do our best to help.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get from Entebbe Airport to Ntinda?

Usually around 1 to 1.5 hours by car. Allow up to 2 hours during weekday morning and evening rush hours.

How much is the Entebbe Expressway toll?

UGX 5,000 per car for a one-way trip, paid in cash in Ugandan shillings at the toll plaza. Rates can change, so carry a little extra.

Does Uber work at Entebbe Airport?

Yes. Uber operates pickups at Entebbe International Airport. Check the app for the current fare estimate and pickup point.

Can you arrange an airport pickup?

Yes, at an extra cost. If you're staying with us, send your flight number and arrival time on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the price and arrange a driver to meet you at arrivals.
