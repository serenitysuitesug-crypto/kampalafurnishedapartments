Why stay in Ntinda?

  • Shops, restaurants and banks close by, so day-to-day errands are easy
  • Quick access to Bukoto, Naguru, Kiwatule and Kisaasi
  • A practical base for business travellers working in north-east Kampala
  • Plenty of transport: taxis, boda bodas and ride-hailing apps work well here

Getting around

From Ntinda you can reach Kampala city centre by road, with travel time depending heavily on traffic, and the Kampala Northern Bypass is close for trips across the city. Entebbe International Airport is roughly 1 to 1.5 hours away by road.

Yes. You get 15% off the nightly rate when you stay 7 nights or more, and 30% off when you stay 30 nights or more. For example, a studio at UGX 100,000 per night costs UGX 85,000 per night on a weekly stay and UGX 70,000 per night on a monthly stay.
