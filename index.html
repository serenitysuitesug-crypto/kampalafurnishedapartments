Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a furnished apartment in Kampala cost per night?

Our studios start from UGX 100,000 per night. One-bedroom apartments are from UGX 160,000 and three-bedroom apartments from UGX 300,000 per night. There are no booking platform fees because you book directly with us.

Which areas of Kampala are your apartments in?

All our apartments are in north-east Kampala: Ntinda, Kisaasi, Kiwatule and Kulambiro.

What is included in the apartments?

Every apartment has free WiFi and a fully equipped kitchen. Other amenities depend on the unit and include daily cleaning, balconies, parking, smart TVs, washing machines and, in one three-bedroom apartment, a swimming pool. Each apartment page lists exactly what it includes.

How do I book?

Send us a WhatsApp message on +256 704 229100 with your dates and the apartment you like, or call +256 778 663411. A local host replies personally to confirm availability and arrange check-in.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 1:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. If you need an earlier check-in or later check-out, message us on WhatsApp and we will do our best to arrange it.

Do you offer discounts for weekly or monthly stays?

Yes. You get 15% off the nightly rate when you stay 7 nights or more, and 30% off when you stay 30 nights or more. For example, a studio at UGX 100,000 per night costs UGX 85,000 per night on a weekly stay and UGX 70,000 per night on a monthly stay.

How far are the apartments from Entebbe Airport?

Entebbe International Airport is roughly 1 to 1.5 hours away by road, depending on traffic. Ask us on WhatsApp if you would like help arranging transport.
