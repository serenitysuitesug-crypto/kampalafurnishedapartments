About this apartment

Stay in Ntinda in this fully furnished studio apartment, set up for comfortable short and long stays.

It comes with free WiFi, balcony view, fully equipped kitchen and daily cleaning, and is priced at UGX 100,000 per night. It suits solo travellers, couples and business visitors, and works well for both short visits and longer stays.

The area: Ntinda

Ntinda is one of Kampala's busiest and best-connected suburbs, in Nakawa Division about 7 km north-east of the city centre. From Ntinda you can reach Kampala city centre by road, with travel time depending heavily on traffic, and the Kampala Northern Bypass is close for trips across the city. Entebbe International Airport is roughly 1 to 1.5 hours away by road. More about staying in Ntinda.
