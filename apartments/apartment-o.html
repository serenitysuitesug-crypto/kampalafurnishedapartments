About this apartment

This one-bedroom apartment in Kisaasi gives you your own private, furnished space with everything you need for the day-to-day.

It comes with free WiFi, smart TV, fully equipped kitchen and ample parking, and is priced at UGX 160,000 per night. It suits couples, remote workers and business travellers, and works well for both short visits and longer stays.

The area: Kisaasi

Kisaasi is a growing residential area in north Kampala, between Bukoto, Kyanja and Kulambiro. Kisaasi connects to Ntinda and Bukoto by road and to the Kampala Northern Bypass for trips across the city. Entebbe International Airport is roughly 1 to 1.5 hours away by road, depending on traffic.
